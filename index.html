Privacy Policy — My Banbox

Effective date: 30 September 2026 Last updated: 30 September 2026

1. Who we are

My Banbox ("the App") is the employee mobile application of the Banbox HR platform. It is published on Google Play by OceanGet ("we", "us"). Your employer decides which information is kept in Banbox HR and is responsible for your employment record; we operate the platform on your employer's behalf.

Contact: contact@banbox.com

2. Who can use the App

The App is only for employees of organisations that use Banbox HR. There is no public registration: your account is created by your employer's HR or admin team. The App is intended for people aged 18 and over.

3. Information we collect

Account and profile information — your name, employee ID, work email, phone number, address, date of birth, national ID number, emergency contact, family details, profile photo and the documents you or your HR team upload (such as ID copies or certificates).

Attendance information — check-in and check-out times and the location of your device at the moment you check in or out. Location is read only while the App is open and you press check in or check out. The App does not track your location in the background.

Leave, loan and other requests — the applications you submit (leave, loan or advance, attendance correction, profile update), their attachments, and the decisions your employer records on them.

Salary information — your payslips, earnings and deductions, and the bank or mobile-banking account you register for salary payment.

Messages — text and voice messages you send in the App's staff chat.

Photos, files and audio — only the photos and documents you choose to attach, and the voice messages you choose to record.

Device and session information — the phone model and operating system, the time of each sign-in, and a push-notification token, so that you can see and sign out the devices connected to your account and receive notifications.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, contacts, SMS, call logs, browsing history or health data. The App contains no advertising.

4. How we use your information

  • to run the App and the HR services your employer uses (attendance, leave, payroll, notices and messaging);
  • to secure your account (sign-in, one-time codes, device sessions);
  • to send you notifications about notices, approvals and messages;
  • to help your employer meet its employment, tax and other legal obligations;
  • to keep the service reliable and fix problems.

5. Sharing

Your information is available to your employer — its HR and administrative staff, according to the permissions your employer configures. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. We use service providers to host the platform and to deliver push notifications (such as Google Firebase Cloud Messaging); they process data only on our instructions. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

6. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Files are kept on access-controlled storage and are served only through short-lived signed links. Sign-in may require a one-time code.

7. How long we keep it

Your employment record is kept for as long as your employer needs it and for as long as the law requires employment, attendance and payroll records to be kept. Device sessions are removed when you sign out or when they expire.

8. Your rights and choices

  • You can view your profile in the App and ask for corrections from Profile → Update request.
  • You can sign out any device from Profile → Your devices.
  • You can refuse location permission, but check-in will then not be possible.
  • To ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or its deletion, contact your employer's HR team or write to contact@banbox.com. We answer within 30 days. Some records cannot be deleted while the law requires your employer to keep them.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page and update the date at the top. Important changes will also be announced in the App.
